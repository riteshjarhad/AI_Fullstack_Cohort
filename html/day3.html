Range Rover Autobiography

Exclusivity & Craftsmanship: Named to reflect a personalized, bespoke experience, the Autobiography trim includes high-end wood veneers, custom paint finishes, and executive-class seating with a Tailgate Event Suite. Performance: Available with potent engine options, such as a 3.0-liter inline-6 petrol/diesel and a massive 4.4-liter twin-turbo V8, alongside plug-in hybrid variants. Pricing & Availability: Because of its top-tier status, it commands a premium. In India, ex-showroom prices for the Autobiography lineup typically range from ₹2.65 Crore to over ₹3.4 Crore, depending on the chosen engine and wheelbase configuration.
